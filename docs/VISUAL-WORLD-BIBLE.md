## 3. Core Pixel-Art Construction Principles

True pixel art is an intentional medium with strict mathematical and visual rules. It is **never** a blurry image with `image-rendering: pixelated` or a smooth vector with a grid overlay.

### A. The Master Pixel Grid & Scale
- **Base Grid Unit:** All environmental elements are aligned to an integer pixel coordinate system.
- **Virtual Resolution:** The world is designed on a base virtual resolution of `480 × 270` (16:9 native pixel buffer) or `640 × 360`, scaled up crisply by integer multiples (`2×`, `3×`, `4×`) using `image-rendering: pixelated` and `image-rendering: crisp-edges`.
- **Consistent Texel Ratio:** Objects across the same visual layer share the exact same pixel density. A character does not have smaller pixels than the ground they walk on.

### B. Pixel Clustering & Stepped Diagonals
- **No Stray Pixels ("Orphan Pixels"):** Isolated single pixels that do not contribute to a highlight or star are forbidden.
- **Intentional Stepped Diagonals:** Slopes, roofs, and swords follow uniform stepping rules (`1:1`, `2:1`, `3:1` staircases).
- **Hard Edges:** No blurred anti-aliasing edges on pixel sprites. Silhouettes are razor-sharp.
- **Banded Dithering:** Gradients across terrain and clouds use ordered Bayer or checkerboard dither clustering rather than smooth continuous blending.

### C. Color Ramps & Palettes
Every material has a restricted color ramp of 3 to 5 shades:
- **Shade 1:** Deep Shadow (often hue-shifted towards cool violet/navy)
- **Shade 2:** Midtone (local color of the material)
- **Shade 3:** Base Light (warm sun/moon wash)
- **Shade 4:** Specular Highlight (crisp 1-2 pixel accent)

## 5. Environmental Anatomy & Layer Separation

Every chapter scene is constructed in **minimum 6 discrete physical planes of depth**:

```
[Layer 0] Celestial Sky (Gradient, Stars, Sun, Moon, Nebula)
    ↓
[Layer 1] Far Clouds & Atmospheric Fog (Slowest drift: 0.05× scroll)
    ↓
[Layer 2] Distant Mountain Ranges & Continental Silhouettes (0.15× scroll)
    ↓
[Layer 3] Midground Architecture (Citadels, Castles, Windmills, Bridges: 0.4× scroll)
    ↓
[Layer 4] Living Ground Plane (Rivers, Roads, Houses, Villagers, Forests: 0.8× scroll)
    ↓
[Layer 5] Interactive Content & Protagonist Plane (DevOps Artifacts, Workshops: 1.0× scroll)
    ↓
[Layer 6] Foreground Framing (Canopy branches, cliff ledges, lanterns, wildflowers: 1.25× scroll)
```

### Landscape Elements Architecture
- **Mountains:** Jagged polygonal rock geometry with stepped lit faces on the lightward side and purple/blue shadow banding on the leeward side. Distant ranges fade into aerial perspective haze.
- **Forests:** Dense clusters of evergreen pine spires and round oak crowns. Top foliage receives warm dapple lighting; base trunks recede into deep forest shadows.
- **Waterways:**
  - Cascading waterfalls: Vertical stepped water columns with white froth splashing at the base.
  - Rivers: Flowing horizontal pixel bands with alternating highlights creating currents.
  - Seas: Rolling swells with breaking whitefoam crests.
- **Architecture:**
  - Castles: Stone blocks with mortar lines, battlements, round turrets, conical tile roofs, arched stained-glass windows.
  - Villages: Timber frame tudor walls, red terracotta or thatch roofs, stone chimneys with puffing smoke sprites, hanging flower boxes.
  - Engineering Citadels: Architectural metaphors for technology (floating sky docks, container crane terminals, aqueduct pipelines, mechanical gearworks).

## 6. The Living World Engine: Characters & Deterministic Life

A static illustration is lifeless. The world breathes through autonomous entities that follow believable state machines without reacting to or chasing the user's cursor:

### A. Independent State Machine Architecture
Each living entity operates on an autonomous loop:
```
[IDLE STATE] (breathing, scanning, shifting weight: 3–6s)
    ↓
[WALK STATE] (steps along defined road coordinates at constant speed)
    ↓
[ACTION STATE] (work with hammer, stir campfire kettle, adjust telescope, sip drink: 4–8s)
    ↓
[PAUSE / TURN STATE] (look left/right, wait for companion)
    ↓
[LOOP REPEAT]
```

### B. Catalog of World Inhabitants
1. **The Watcher (Cosmos):** Adjusts telescope, turns towards lantern, looks up into the stars.
2. **The Traveling Knight & Horchato the Goat (Daybreak Meadows):** Knight rests with heavy travel pack; goat occasionally chews grass, wiggles ears, and tugs the rope.
3. **The Dockworkers & Airship Riggers (Engineering Harbor):** Lifting container crates, uncoiling mooring ropes, signaling incoming zeppelins.
4. **The Council of Architects (Tavern & Guildhall):** Sitting around a stone table, studying blueprints, gesturing with mugs.
5. **Doug the Stone Golem (Campfire Clearing):** Sitting peacefully beside the traveler, steam rising from his wooden cup, cyan eyes blinking gently.
6. **Wildlife & Atmosphere:**
   - Flocks of 3-5 birds flying horizontally across high skies.
   - Fireflies drifting with organic Perlin-noise float patterns.
   - Campfire sparks ascending and fading into the night air.
   - Windmills rotating at steady, hypnotic angular velocities.

## 9. Technical Architecture & Performance Strategy

### Framework & Rendering Engine
- **Next.js 16 (App Router) + TypeScript:** Semantic SSR for SEO and baseline accessibility.
- **Hybrid Rendering Architecture:**
  - **Procedural Canvas 2D Buffers:** Used for starfields, weather particles (rain, fireflies, embers), and flowing river currents where running DOM elements would cause high frame drops.
  - **Crisp SVG Pixel-Art Composables:** Used for buildings, characters, terrain layers, and interactive structures. Vector pixel SVGs retain 100% pixel crispness at any DPR or zoom level with zero blur.
  - **CSS Stepped Transforms:** Keyframe animations utilize `steps()` timing functions to preserve retro pixel cadence (e.g. `steps(4)` for walking cycles).
- **Audio Architecture:**
  - HTML5 Web Audio API engine encapsulated in React Context.
  - Smooth volume fading across scenes.
  - Autoplay-safe initialization unlocked on first user interaction.
  - Non-intrusive floating HUD controller with track selector.
